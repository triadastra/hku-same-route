## How concentrated the choices are

- The 15 most visited places (1% of the map) take 37.4% of the free trips; the top 10% take 78.3%.
- 29.3% of the places were never chosen by anyone.
- Free trips walked by the quickest path: median 404 m, middle half 178 to 733 m. 31.7% of the free trips are strolls with no fixed route.
